# MockForge Brand Identity

## Logo

The MockForge logo represents the core concept of "forging" APIs and mock backends through a development tool.

### Symbolism
- **Anvil**: Represents the foundation and strength of the platform
- **Hammer**: Symbolizes the tool that shapes and creates
- **API Lines**: Connection lines representing REST endpoints
- **Blue/Cyan Dots**: Input (requests)
- **Green Dots**: Output (responses)
- **Sparks**: Active development and creation process

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Direct**: Clear, concise explanations without fluff
- **Technical**: Accurate terminology for developer audience
- **Empowering**: "You can build this" rather than "We'll do it for you"
- **Pragmatic**: Focus on practical solutions over theoretical perfection

### Examples

✅ **Good**: "Define a resource. Generate mock data. Call the API."

❌ **Avoid**: "Leverage our revolutionary paradigm-shifting platform to synergize your development workflow."

✅ **Good**: "MockForge runs locally. No account required."

❌ **Avoid**: "Experience the freedom of our cutting-edge local-first architecture."

## UI Principles

### Design System
1. **Density**: Information-dense but not cluttered
2. **Hierarchy**: Clear visual hierarchy with typography and spacing
3. **Feedback**: Immediate visual feedback for all actions
4. **Consistency**: Reusable components with consistent behavior

### Component Style
- **Borders**: 1-2px, subtle (#0f3460)
- **Border Radius**: 4px standard, 8px for cards
- **Shadows**: Minimal, only for elevation
- **Spacing**: 8px grid system (8, 16, 24, 32, 48)

---

## Icon Style

### Characteristics
- **Stroke**: 2px line weight
- **Style**: Outlined, not filled (except active states)
- **Grid**: 24x24px canvas
- **Corners**: Rounded (2px radius)

### Icon Sources
- [Lucide Icons](https://lucide.dev/) (primary)
- [Heroicons](https://heroicons.com/) (fallback)

## Asset Guidelines

### Logo Usage
- **Minimum Size**: 40px height
- **Clear Space**: 10px minimum around logo
- **Backgrounds**: Use on dark (#1a1a2e) or light (#ffffff) backgrounds
- **Do Not**: Distort, recolor, rotate, or add effects

### Screenshots
- **Format**: PNG with transparency or JPG
- **Resolution**: 2x retina (e.g., 1600x900 displayed at 800x450)
- **Annotations**: Use coral red (#e94560) for highlights
